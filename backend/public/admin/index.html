Quick Answers & Platform Governance

✅ Buyer & Seller Accountability: Complete history is recorded for every order with unit prices frozen at the time of purchase.

✅ Stock Race Condition Protection: Atomic PostgreSQL transactions prevent negative inventory on simultaneous orders.

✅ Buyer Acceptance: Confirmed receipts store an immutable accepted_at database timestamp.

✅ Dispute Resolution: End-to-end complaint lifecycles with message threads between buyer, seller, and admin.
